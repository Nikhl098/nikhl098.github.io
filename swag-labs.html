BUG-01MajorInventory

Product images broken for problem_user

Steps to reproduce

  1. Log in as problem_user
  2. View the inventory page

Expected

Each product displays its own product image.

Actual

All 6 product images load the same placeholder file. No real product image is shown.

BUG-02MajorSorting

Product sorting does not work for problem_user

Steps to reproduce

  1. Log in as problem_user
  2. In “Sort products”, select “Name (Z to A)”

Expected

Product list reorders from Z to A and the selection stays.

Actual

The selection immediately reverts to “Name (A to Z)” and the order never changes.
