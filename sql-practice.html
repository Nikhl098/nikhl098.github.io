Topics covered

  • Aggregates: COUNT, SUM, AVG, MIN, MAX, GROUP BY, HAVING
  • Joins: INNER JOIN, LEFT JOIN, joining three tables
  • Subqueries and CASE: NOT IN, second highest value, status percentage
  • Tester checks: duplicates, missing values, invalid emails, orphan records, negative stock, order total vs item total, business rule violations
