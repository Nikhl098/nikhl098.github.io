How I test

Four habits I bring to every build.

Understand the flow

Read the requirement, map the happy path, then list what a real user might do instead.

Design positive & negative cases

Every case has clear steps, test data and an expected result.

Execute and keep evidence

Actual results recorded honestly, with screenshots or responses as proof.

Report so it gets fixed

Severity, steps to reproduce, expected vs actual, reproduced before it’s logged.
