About the studio

The chair behind 6000+ bridal mornings.

Beauty Town Makeup Studio is led by Mohini Mishra, a professional makeup artist in Civil Lines, Etawah — trusted for bridal, engagement, party and festive looks, and hair treatments.

01 / How a booking goes

Every look, planned before it is applied.

Step 1

Consultation

We talk about your occasion, outfit, jewellery and the finish you like — natural, soft glam or full bridal.

Step 2

Look planning

Shades, base and hairstyle are decided for your skin tone and the lighting of your venue.

Step 3

Skin & hair prep

Prep guidance and, where needed, a hair treatment so everything sits perfectly on the day.

Step 4

The reveal

Makeup, hair, draping and setting — finished on time, ready for the camera and the long day ahead.
